# Ears — PyAutoEars

The **Ears** listen to public community conversations and feedback. The organ
owns read-only collection, the versioned community snapshot, coverage receipts
and the [listening board](https://pyautolabs.github.io/PyAutoEars/).

**Repo:** [PyAutoLabs/PyAutoEars](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/PyAutoEars)

GitHub conversations remain authoritative. Brain’s Community conductor reads
the snapshot, judges what needs attention, drafts replies for human approval
and routes accepted development through Mind. Ears never posts replies, labels
or issues; Mind remains the owner of task state.

The board distinguishes observed attention, incomplete coverage and stale
observations. Empty or unavailable coverage never means no work. Public source
metadata is rendered inertly; no raw bodies, transcripts or private sources
are published. The cockpit reads its `state.json` feed in canonical organ order,
after Eyes. The snapshot contract and operational commands are documented in
[REFERENCE.md](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/PyAutoEars/blob/main/REFERENCE.md).

`/feedback` helps people or their agents draft a reviewed usage report for the
existing Discussions hub. Follow-through tracking and recurring-theme synthesis
remain later phases of `community-organ-birth`; they are not implied by this
collector or its dashboard.
