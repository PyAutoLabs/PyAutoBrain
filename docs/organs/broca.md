# Broca — PyAutoBroca

**What it owns:** the evidence on how the AI assistants are doing. Broca keeps
assistant evaluation history, upkeep evidence, collection receipts and the
assistant upkeep board.

**Repo:** [PyAutoLabs/PyAutoBroca](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/PyAutoBroca)

## The defining function: watching the assistants speak

Broca's area is the part of the brain tied to producing language. This organ
watches the organism's public assistants and records how well they work.
Benchmarks and maintenance inventory are kept as separate kinds of evidence,
and a missing or incomparable result never counts as a pass.

## The boundary that matters

The public assistants stay independent. They keep their own definitions and
reproducible runners, anyone can use them on their own, and they never depend
on Broca.

Broca records; it does not judge or act.

- **Brain** interprets the evidence and routes accepted changes through Mind.
- **Mind** tracks the fixes.
- **Cortex** keeps science records and **Heart** keeps readiness, as everywhere
  else.

Broca is a public repository, so everything committed to it must be suitable
for publication. Raw transcripts and private project data are excluded. It
does not launch model or HPC campaigns on its own, and its Actions workflow
publishes only the rendered saved snapshot, without collecting new evidence.

## For an adopter

Broca is an **instance organ**: its evidence is about *your* assistants. If you
publish assistants of your own, create a Broca with the same shape. The full
boundary is the
[Broca section of ORGANISM.md](https://github.com/PyAutoLabs/PyAutoBrain/blob/main/ORGANISM.md#broca--assistant-evidence).
